# Bike Sales Data Cleaning & Excel Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates an end-to-end Business Analyst workflow using a bike sales dataset and Microsoft Excel. The analysis begins with data cleaning and preparation, followed by PivotTable-based analysis of customer characteristics and purchasing behavior. The findings are then presented through an Excel dashboard designed to communicate key business metrics and customer patterns clearly.

The project demonstrates how raw customer data can be transformed into structured analysis and business-focused reporting.

## Business Objective

The objective is to understand the characteristics associated with bike purchases and develop an Excel dashboard that enables stakeholders to explore customer segments, purchasing patterns, and key sales indicators.

## Analytical Approach

* Cleaned and prepared the raw bike sales dataset.
* Reviewed customer and purchasing variables for consistency and analysis.
* Created PivotTables to summarize purchasing behavior across key customer segments.
* Analyzed purchasing patterns by income, gender, age group, age, and commute distance.
* Calculated purchase counts and segment-level patterns.
* Developed an Excel dashboard using KPIs, charts, and visual summaries.
* Translated the analysis into business-focused insights.

## Key Findings

### Overall Purchase Performance

The dataset contains **1,000 customers**, of whom **481 purchased a bike**, resulting in an overall purchase rate of **48.1%**.

### Income and Bike Purchase

Customers who purchased a bike had a higher average income than customers who did not:

| Purchase Status  | Average Income |
| ---------------- | -------------: |
| Purchased Bike   |        $57,963 |
| Did Not Purchase |        $54,875 |

This indicates an association between higher income levels and bike purchase behavior within the dataset.

### Age Segment

The middle-age segment represents the largest customer group, with **701 customers**.

Among this segment:

* **383 purchased a bike**
* **318 did not purchase**
* Purchase rate: approximately **54.6%**

By comparison, the adolescent and older segments had lower purchase rates of approximately **35.5%** and **31.2%**, respectively.

### Commute Distance

Purchase behavior also varies across commute-distance groups.

The highest purchase rates were observed among customers commuting **2–5 miles (58.6%)** and **0–1 mile (54.6%)**.

Purchase rates declined for longer commutes, reaching approximately **29.7%** among customers commuting more than 10 miles.

These patterns can help identify customer segments that may warrant closer attention in marketing and sales planning.

## Dashboard

The Excel dashboard consolidates the analysis into a visual reporting format, allowing users to review key customer and purchasing patterns without working directly with the underlying PivotTables.

The dashboard is designed to support:

* Customer segmentation
* Purchase behavior analysis
* KPI monitoring
* Comparative analysis across customer groups
* Business reporting and decision support

## Business Implications

The analysis suggests several areas that could support business decision-making:

* **Middle-age customers** represent a large and relatively high-purchase segment and may be an important target market.
* Customers in the **0–5 mile commute range** show comparatively strong purchase rates.
* **Income segmentation** may help identify customer groups with greater purchasing potential.
* Lower purchase rates among older and longer-distance commuter segments may warrant further investigation before developing targeted marketing strategies.

These findings represent **observed patterns and associations** in the dataset rather than causal relationships.
